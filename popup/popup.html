Unit Price Sorter

v1.0

Re-sorts grocery listings by their true unit price (per lb, per fl oz, or per each) instead of the sticker price.

  1. Open a grocery store page on Uber Eats.
  2. Find the floating Unit Price Sorter panel (bottom-right).
  3. Click “Sort by unit price.” The page auto-scrolls to load every item, then groups and ranks them.
  4. Use Order to flip cheapest-first / priciest-first.
